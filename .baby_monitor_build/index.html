    How Little Days calculates this

    References are encoded locally from the American Academy of Pediatrics formula guidance, AAP newborn assessment guidance, CDC formula guidance, CDC newborn breastfeeding guidance, and Academy of Breastfeeding Medicine Protocol #3.

    Reference ranges describe typical feeding patterns and are not individualized medical prescriptions.

    Little Days cannot diagnose feeding problems. Breastfeeding minutes are never converted into milk volume. Formula should always be prepared exactly as directed by its manufacturer unless a clinician gives an individualized recipe.
