RESEARCH STORIES

Scientific Blog

Long-form perspectives on neuroimaging, trustworthy AI, multimodal learning, medical imaging, and intelligent sensing.

Research rarely fits neatly into a figure, metric, or publication abstract. These articles explore the questions behind my work: how artificial intelligence can learn from brain imaging and clinical data, how models behave across populations, how multiple information sources can be combined, and how computational methods can reveal meaningful patterns in complex biomedical data.

Each article connects several related publications into a broader scientific story—explaining the problem, the computational ideas, what the studies found, what remains uncertain, and where the research may go next.

Dementia & Trustworthy AI

Understanding dementia. Predicting what comes next.

From detecting dementia-related patterns to estimating disease progression and examining fairness across populations, this research explores how multimodal AI can learn from incomplete and heterogeneous biomedical data while asking an equally important question: for whom does the model work?

Neuroimaging · Multimodal Learning · Trustworthy AI · Fairness
2018–2026 · 5 journal papersRead research story →
Brain Aging & Vascular Health

Brain aging, beyond a single biomarker.

Brain aging is reflected in networks, anatomy, vascular health, and population-level variation. This research connects machine-learning biomarkers derived from large functional-connectome datasets with clinical studies of cerebral small-vessel disease and blood-pressure control.

Connectomics · Brain Aging · Vascular Health · Population Studies
2026–2026 · 2 journal papersRead research story →
Multimodal Emotion & Social AI

Understanding emotion in context.

A facial expression, a voice, or a sentence rarely tells the whole story. This research investigates how AI can combine audio, visual, linguistic, temporal, and relational information to understand emotion in conversations and social interactions.

Graph Learning · Multimodal AI · Emotion · Social Context
2020–2024 · 6 journal papersRead research story →
Medical Image Analysis

From an image to a meaningful region.

When labeled medical images are limited, knowing where a model should look can be as important as the classifier itself. This work connects segmentation, attention, reconstruction, and semi-supervised learning to extract more meaningful representations from medical images.

Medical Imaging · Segmentation · Semi-supervised Learning · Attention
2019–2020 · 2 journal papersRead research story →
Mobile Sensing & Intelligent Interaction

Turning everyday signals into intelligent interaction.

Smartphones, depth cameras, and wireless systems all operate with imperfect measurements. These projects explore how computational models can convert noisy movement, visual, and signal data into localization estimates and natural human-computer interactions.

Mobile Sensing · Human Interaction · Computer Vision · Signal Processing
